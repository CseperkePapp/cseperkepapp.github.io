A quiet essay

On the pleasure of a line arriving.

Reading is a kind of choreography. Each line waits its turn, then lifts into place — and if the timing is right, you never notice the mechanism at all.

The principle

Nothing appears. Everything emerges.

A word that simply blinks into being feels cheap. A word that rises from beneath a hidden edge feels considered — earned. The mask does the work; the reader feels only grace.

On restraint

Slow is a luxury.

The easing curve lingers at the end — a long, soft settle — so the motion reads as calm rather than eager. Confidence is measured in how little you rush.

Type in motion should feel like breathing, not blinking.
